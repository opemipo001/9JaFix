About Us

9JaFix connects drivers and riders with trusted mechanics near them, in minutes. Whether your car won't start on the expressway, your bike breaks down in traffic, or you have a flat tyre far from home, help shouldn't be hard to find. With 9JaFix, you share your location, see mechanics nearby, and get help from someone who can actually reach you.

Founded by Bankole, a Final-Year Student in the Department of Computer Science, Kwara State University (KWASU), Malete, Kwara State, Nigeria. We connect people with reliable mechanics quickly, clearly, and fairly, so finding help feels as simple as ordering a ride. Whether you drive a car, ride a bike, or run a workshop, 9JaFix is built to get you where you need to be: back on the road, fast. We help clients get help from nearby mechanics.

What We Stand For

  • Speed. Request help in a few taps and the mechanic gets in touch with you directly
  • Trust. Real profiles, real reviews, and mechanics checked before they're listed
  • Fair dealing. Clear information about who you're contacting and what they offer
  • Local roots. Built for Nigerian roads, Nigerian drivers, and the mechanics who keep us moving.

Meet Bankole Abdulsalam

Bankole Abdulsalam is a Frontend Developer and final-year Computer Science student at Kwara State University (KWASU), Malete, Nigeria. With a growing passion for software development, technology, and digital innovation, he is focused on building practical and intuitive digital solutions that address real-world problems.

He is the founder of 9JaFix, a platform designed to make finding reliable automotive assistance easier, faster, and more accessible. Recognising the challenges drivers and riders face when their vehicles break down or require maintenance, he set out to create a solution that connects people with nearby mechanics without the usual stress and uncertainty.

Whether it's a car owner stranded on the roadside, a motorcyclist in need of repairs, or a mechanic looking to connect with more customers, 9JaFix aims to bring both sides together through a simple, accessible, and reliable experience. Through 9JaFix, Bankole combines his knowledge of frontend development, passion for problem-solving, and interest in emerging technologies to build solutions that simplify everyday challenges and create meaningful value for people.
